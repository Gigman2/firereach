## Source and licensing

- Coordinates and station names originate from OpenStreetMap
  (`amenity=fire_station`), which is licensed **ODbL (Open Database
  License)**. Region and district come from OSM Nominatim reverse geocoding,
  also ODbL.
- The ODbL requires **attribution** and is **share-alike**: databases
  produced by extracting/reshaping ODbL data are themselves derivative
  databases and inherit the share-alike obligation. This bundle is such a
  derived database. Whether that obligation is satisfied for a shipped
  product is a licensing decision for the product owner, not something this
  generator resolves.

## Data-quality caveats (carried from the seed's own header)

- `responseRate` encodes **publication order** on the GNFS contact-numbers
  page, not measured or reported reliability. The field name overstates what
  the values mean.
- Contact numbers are **regional command lines, not per-station direct
  lines**, and were archived from the GNFS site on **2022-08-09**. They may
  be stale.
- **NOT VERIFIED FOR EMERGENCY USE.** Confirm every number directly with
  GNFS before any production/emergency-facing deployment.
